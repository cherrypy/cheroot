Fixed every :py:class:`~cheroot.server.HTTPServer` staying alive with its
gateway for the life of the process, stopped ones and ones whose
:py:meth:`~cheroot.server.HTTPServer.prepare` failed or never ran included:
``logging.statistics`` now holds a server's statistics only while it serves.
Retired worker threads no longer stay in their server's statistics, even
when statistics are enabled. Their counters and throughput contributions
are retained in bounded totals, while ``Worker Threads`` lists only live
records.
