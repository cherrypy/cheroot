Fixed ``logging.statistics`` retaining
:py:class:`~cheroot.server.HTTPServer` instances and their gateways for the
life of the process, including servers that had stopped, failed to start,
or never started. Server statistics are now registered after
:py:meth:`~cheroot.server.HTTPServer.prepare` succeeds and removed by
:py:meth:`~cheroot.server.HTTPServer.stop`.

Worker threads that have exited no longer stay in their server's
``Worker Threads`` statistics, and their counts still add to the server's
totals.
